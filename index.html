Our Mission

1 in 10 British children do not own a single book in their home
22% Average gap behind classmates in core subjects by age 11
500+ Books collected in our first drive for a local community library

Currently 1 in 10 British children do not own a single book in their home, and by age 11 disadvantaged pupils are at an average of 22% behind their classmates in core subjects. This has a real knock-on effect to success in public exams and their working life, as young people without support find themselves systematically shut out of high paying jobs. This furthers the generational cycle of a worsening literacy gap, or the disparity in reading and writing levels between people from socioeconomically disadvantaged backgrounds and their peers.

At Literacy Bridge, we aim to help with levelling the playing field, and we are thrilled to share that after our first book drive, over 500 books were collected and are being distributed to a local community library, marking our first small contribution to this important cause.
