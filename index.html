Who We Are

Literacy Bridge is a local initiative started by friends passionate about making books accessible to everyone. We collect gently used books from generous neighbours and redistribute them to local schools, community centres, and families.

What We Collect

Yes, Please!

  • Children's & young adult books
  • Modern fiction & non-fiction
  • Clean, intact paperbacks & hardbacks
  • Recent study & revision guides

No, Thank You

  • Torn, water-damaged, or mouldy books
  • Outdated encyclopedias or dictionaries
  • Magazines, catalogues, or newspapers
  • Books with heavy writing/highlighting
