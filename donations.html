Book Donation Guidelines

Books We Accept

  • Children's picture books and early readers
  • Young adult and teen fiction
  • Clean, intact adult fiction and non-fiction
  • Recent GCSE, A-Level, and study guides

Items We Cannot Accept

  • Torn, water-damaged, or mouldy books
  • Outdated encyclopedias and reference sets
  • Magazines, catalogues, or newspapers
  • Workbooks with heavy writing or highlighting

Arrange a Drop-Off or Group Collection

If you have a large box of books to donate outside of our scheduled drive dates, or would like Literacy Bridge to host a collection at your school or workplace, please get in touch with our team.
